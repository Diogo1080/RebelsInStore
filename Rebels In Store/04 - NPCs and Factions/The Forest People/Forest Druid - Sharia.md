# Sharia

A druid of the forest people, the lizardfolk who watch the colony from the trees. Tall and grey-green, with a crest of white feathers and yellow eyes, she carries a staff hung with small bones. The party meets her in [[02 - The Lean Weeks]].

## Background
- She tends the sacred places of her people: the [[01 - The Water Survey#4. The Stone Spring|Stone Spring]], the marker posts and the old orchard.
- She has watched the colony since the day it landed. She was the one who tied the cloths to the post and changed the offerings at the spring.
- Her people remember "the pale ones" who came from the sea long ago, planted the orchard and grew old here. She knows their story, and she's deciding whether the colony is the same kind of people or a worse one.

## Personality
- **Patient and watchful.** She lets people come to her, and she lets them talk first.
- **Speaks little.** Draconic, and a few words of Common, in short, careful phrases: *"You come... to stay? Or to take?"*
- **Reads people well.** She notices fear, greed and lies, and says so plainly (*"Salt-one... is afraid. Fear... grows teeth."*).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Ideal** | What is taken must be given back |
| **Bond** | Her people's sacred places, and the trees the pale ones left behind |
| **Flaw** | Slow to trust, and slower to forgive. She never forgets an insult |

## Ambitions
- Learn whether the colony will **share** the land or **take** it.
- Keep the colony away from the Stone Spring and the hunting grounds, unless it asks.
- Find out what happened to the old camp's settlers, and who among the newcomers is dangerous.

## Attitude to the Colony
How Sharia treats the colony follows the forest people's attitude (see [[Colony Production#Either Way|Signs from the trees]]):

| Attitude | Sharia… |
| --- | --- |
| **Hostile** | Warns them off, and fights if they cross her line |
| **Wary** | Talks, asks questions and judges the answers |
| **Friendly** | Talks, helps, and shares what the forest gives |

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: Secret
> Sharia saw [[Navigator - Corwin|Ashby]] sketching the rock face at the spring, and she watched him go through the colony's camp at night. She doesn't know what a spy is, but she knows a thief when she sees one.
> - If the party earns her trust, she warns them: *"The map-one... takes, and hides what he takes."*
> - She would rather deal with the party than with Halloway, whose soldiers carry steel everywhere.

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: The pale ones
> The northern family who built the keeper's house didn't die out. They **joined Sharia's people**: married in, learned their ways, and were buried as the forest people bury their dead, so there are no graves to find. Some of the forest people still carry their blood, and a few of their words.
> - Sharia knows the whole story. She may be descended from them herself *(to be decided)*.
> - She won't tell it to anyone who hasn't earned her trust, because she fears the Crown would come to claim them, or their land.

## In Combat
She fights only to get away. She uses *Entangle* to hold attackers, then *Pass without Trace* and Forest Step to vanish into the trees. If she's attacked, the forest people's attitude drops to **Hostile**.

## Loot
Her staff of bones, a pouch of herbs and seeds, and a necklace of carved wooden tokens (the same marks as the spring). Taking any of it, if she's killed, is an insult her people will not forgive.

### Design Notes
- A house-made NPC built from the 2024 Monster Manual *Druid* and lizardfolk traits, checked against the DMG's CR guidelines. It is not an official stat block.
- **Defence:** 58 HP and AC 14 sit around CR 2–3.
- **Offence:** two staff hits average about 15 damage a round; *Moonbeam* adds sustained damage, putting her around CR 3.
- She is a hard fight for three level-2 or level-3 characters, and she doesn't fight to the death.
