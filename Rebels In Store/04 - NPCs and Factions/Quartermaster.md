# Quartermaster Isolde Brannock

The expedition's quartermaster. She is [[Expedition Commander|Commander Halloway]]'s oldest friend and his right arm.

## Background
- Served as purser aboard [[The Royal Fortune]] during the wars, keeping the gun deck fed, supplied and paid while Halloway kept it firing.
- They have known each other for over twenty years. She once got the crew through a blockade on half rations, and he has trusted her with every ledger and every secret since.
- She asked to join the expedition. Halloway didn't have to ask her.

## Role in the Colony
- Manages the colony's stores, rations and inventory; she is the in-game face of [[Colony Production]] and [[Expedition Supplies]].
- Keeps the books for [[Royal Supply Contracts]], [[Crown Exports]] and the Royal Tax.
- **Second in command.** When Halloway is away, she speaks with his authority.

## Personality
- **Sharp, dry and impossible to fool.** She knows exactly how many barrels are in the hold and who took the missing one.
- **Halloway's conscience.** She is the only person who can tell him he's wrong to his face, and she does it in private.
- **Protective.** Of him first, then of the stores, then of everyone else.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Ideal** | Nobody starves on my watch |
| **Bond** | Edric Halloway, whom she would follow anywhere |
| **Flaw** | She distrusts anyone who wastes supplies, and holds grudges about it |

## Relationship with the Party
- **Best ally for getting things done.** Players who earn her respect get honest numbers, early warnings about shortages and a word in Halloway's ear.
- **Worst enemy to cross.** She notices theft, smuggling and cooked books immediately. Selling exports behind the Crown's back would be very hard without her.

> [!tip] Campaign hook
> She is the key to Halloway. Winning her over is the surest way to bring him round to the colony's side. Turning her against the party pushes him firmly back towards the Crown.
