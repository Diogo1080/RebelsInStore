# Aldous Fenwick

Leader of [[The Agricultural Settlers]]. A big, quiet tenant farmer in his forties with soil permanently under his fingernails.

## Background
- His family worked the same fields for five generations, as tenants of a lord who never set foot on them. When the lord sold the land for sheep pasture, the Fenwicks were evicted with a week's notice.
- He signed up for the expedition because the Crown promised **land grants** to settlers who work the land for seven years.
- The other farmers chose him to speak for them because he's honest, not because he wanted to.

## Personality
- **Patient, slow to speak, stubborn as an ox.** He thinks in seasons, not days.
- **Quietly bitter about nobility.** He's polite to officers, but he never forgets who owns what.
- **Kind to animals and children.** He'll be the first to ask about the bear in [[01 - The Water Survey]].

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Ideal** | A man should own the land he works |
| **Bond** | The promise of a land deed with his family's name on it |
| **Flaw** | Sees every tree as a field waiting to happen |

## Ambitions
- **Clear the forest** for fields and pasture as fast as possible.
- Get the land grants written down and signed by [[Expedition Commander - Edric|Halloway]], not just promised.
- Build a [[Crown Exports|mill and granary]].

## Political Stance
- **Rivals:** [[Coastal Leader - Marta|Marta]], who wants to stay on the coast. His forest clearing will also clash with whoever leaves the offerings at the stones.
- **Crown:** loyal for now, but only because of the land grants. If the Crown breaks that promise, he becomes one of the **first and most determined rebels**.

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: Secret
> Fenwick has no dark secret. His weakness is the **land grant**. The Crown never intended to honour it in full: the deeds will arrive saying the land belongs to the Crown, "leased" to the settlers.
> - This is a great mid-campaign turning point. The day the deeds arrive, the farmers turn on the Crown.

## In Combat
Use the **Commoner** stat block (2024 Monster Manual), with a pitchfork. He's not a fighter, but he's strong: give him Advantage on Strength checks.

## Loot
Nothing of value beyond their work clothes and tools.
