# Navigator Corwin Ashby

The expedition's navigator: soft-spoken, scholarly and always sketching coastlines.

## Public Face (what the party knows)
- A royal cartographer assigned to [[The Royal Expedition]] by the Admiralty. He holds the charts and instruments of [[The Royal Fortune]].
- Charming, curious and helpful. He offers to map the land around the colony, keeps detailed journals and asks a lot of questions.
- A newcomer: neither [[Expedition Commander - Edric|Halloway]] nor [[Quartermaster - Isolde|Brannock]] had met him before the voyage.

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: The Truth
> Ashby is the **Crown's spy**, placed in the expedition to watch the colony and its leaders, Halloway included.
> - **Mission:** report on loyalty, wealth, hidden resources and any sign of independence.
> - **Method:** his "navigation journals" are coded reports. He sends them home in sealed Admiralty dispatch cases on every [[Royal Supply Contracts|supply ship]], and the crew never question them.
> - **Influence:** his reports directly feed the Crown's attitude. Anything the players hide that he discovers ends up in the Crown's hands, and can raise the Royal Tax or Crown distrust.
> - **Neither Halloway nor Brannock knows.** The Crown doesn't fully trust its own war hero either.
>
> **Why he will always stay in the colony:** if [[The Royal Fortune]] sails home, Ashby insists on staying behind "to chart the coast". This is an early clue.

## Opinions
- **Dismantling [[The Royal Fortune]]:** he's openly enthusiastic and praises it as "bold and practical".

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: Why he really likes it
> Without its own ship, the colony depends entirely on the Crown's supply ships. It can't flee, trade on its own or contact anyone else. A navigator with no ship to navigate should also raise suspicion about why he is still here. If he pushes for this hard, it's a clue.

## Clues for the Party
- He's always there when something valuable is found.
- His journals contain far more about people than about stars or tides.
- He reacts badly when anyone handles the sealed dispatch cases.
- A sailor notices he navigates surprisingly poorly for a royal navigator.
- [[Quartermaster - Isolde|Brannock]] finds supplies on her ledgers that she never ordered: sealing wax, cipher paper, extra ink.
- He asks probing questions about Halloway's opinions of the Crown.

## If He Is Exposed
- **Halloway** is enraged at the Crown's distrust. It's a big push towards the colony's side.
- **The Crown** loses its eyes in the colony, but notices when the reports stop arriving.
- **The players' choice:** arrest him, kill him, turn him into a double agent who feeds the Crown false reports, or quietly let him carry on.
