# Mother Agathe Morrow

Leader of [[The Religious Order]]. A calm, grey-haired priestess in her late fifties with a healer's hands and a judge's eyes.

## Background
- Ran a hospice in the capital's slums for twenty years, through two plagues.
- She asked the church to send her to the new continent. Some say it was a calling; others say the bishops wanted her criticism of the court far away.
- She is the colony's only real physician. Everyone will owe her their life sooner or later.

## Personality
- **Gentle, patient and unshakeable.** She never raises her voice and never needs to.
- **Morally uncompromising.** She'll heal anyone, but she'll tell them exactly what she thinks of them while doing it.
- **Politically sharp.** She knows how to use the church's weight against the Crown when she needs to.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Ideal** | Mercy for all, without exception |
| **Bond** | The sick and the poor, wherever she finds them |
| **Flaw** | Certain she knows what's best for everyone, including people who never asked |

## Ambitions
- **Build a temple and infirmary**, with the best stone available.
- **Reach the local people.** She believes the people leaving offerings at the stones need to be met with kindness, and converted.
- Collect a tithe from the colony's output to fund the Order.

## Political Stance
- **Rivals:** [[Alchemist Leader - Octavia|Doctor Lisle]] (heresy, and competition over healing) and [[Industrial Leader - Hilde|Vossberg]] (the stone).
- **Crown:** she answers to the **church**, not the Crown. If the Crown treats the colonists cruelly, she'll side with the colony, and her word carries weight back home.

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: Secret
> The church sent her with orders of its own: report on the colony to the bishops, just as [[Navigator - Corwin|Ashby]] reports to the Crown.
> - Her reports are honest, and she'll protect the colonists in them.
> - She suspects Ashby. If the party earns her trust, she can be their **first ally against the spy**, and she knows how sealed dispatches are handled.
> - Her wish to convert the locals is the real danger. Handled badly, it could start the colony's first conflict with the people of the land.

## In Combat
Use the **Priest** stat block (2024 Monster Manual). She only fights to protect the wounded, and prefers healing and *Sanctuary* to attacking.
