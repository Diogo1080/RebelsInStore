# The Industrial Settlers

Craftsmen, builders and skilled workers recruited to build the infrastructure of a permanent colony. Practical people who know a settlement cannot live in tents forever. See [[Settler Groups]].

## Composition (original 20-person draft)

| Profession | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Carpenters | 5 |
| Blacksmiths | 3 |
| Masons | 3 |
| General builders | 4 |
| Wheelwrights and toolmakers | 2 |
| Labourers | 3 |
| **Total** | **20** |

## Advantages
- Permanent housing and defensive structures
- Repair tools and basic equipment
- Workshops and production facilities
- Process local timber and stone
- Less dependence on imported manufactured goods

## Disadvantages
- Consume a lot of food
- Require tools, materials and equipment
- Produce little food or income directly
- Need iron, timber, stone and other raw materials

## Long-term Opportunities
Construction services, furniture and tools, processed timber, metalworking, shipbuilding and infrastructure.

## Export
**Sawmill → Ship timber and masts.** The Royal Navy always needs tall, straight timber. See [[Crown Exports]].

## Crown Sells Them
Advanced tools, metal ingots, machinery, specialist equipment, architectural plans. See [[Royal Supply Contracts]].
