# The Agricultural Settlers

Farmers, herders, gardeners and agricultural workers, many from overcrowded rural communities, who see the continent as a chance to own land. See [[Settler Groups]].

**Leader:** [[Agricultural Leader - Aldous|Aldous Fenwick]]

## Composition

| Profession | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Farmers | 8 |
| Herders | 4 |
| Gardeners and seed specialists | 3 |
| Animal handlers | 3 |
| Agricultural labourers | 2 |
| **Total** | **20** |

## Production
A Food bonus on top of the colony's base 1, by season. Year 1: **+0** in spring, **+1** in summer, **+2** in autumn, **+0** in winter. From year 2 onwards the fields are established: **+1** in spring, **+2** in summer, **+3** in autumn, **+0** in winter. See [[Colony Production]].

## Advantages
- Crop fields and vegetable gardens
- Raise livestock if suitable animals exist
- Soil and irrigation management
- Long-term food surpluses
- Orchards and plantations

## Disadvantages
- Agriculture takes time to become productive
- Needs tools, seeds, livestock and cleared land
- Vulnerable to poor harvests, pests and weather
- Initially depends on imported food

## Long-term Opportunities
Grain, livestock and animal products, fruit and vegetable exports, brewing and food processing, commercial plantations.

## Export
**Mill and Granary → Flour and grain.** Grain feeds the Crown's cities and armies. See [[Crown Exports]].

## Crown Sells Them
Seeds, livestock, ploughs, hoes, irrigation equipment, mills, agricultural expertise. See [[Royal Supply Contracts]].
