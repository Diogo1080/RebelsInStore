# The Coastal Settlers

Experienced fishermen, sailors, coastal gatherers and people used to living off the sea. They believe the coast is the safest place to settle and the ocean can provide everything the colony initially needs. See [[Settler Groups]].

## Composition (original 20-person draft)

| Profession | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Experienced fishermen | 8 |
| Sailors and boatmen | 4 |
| Coastal gatherers | 5 |
| Fish processors and preservers | 3 |
| **Total** | **20** |

## Advantages
- Reliable fishing industry
- Identify edible shellfish, seaweed and coastal resources
- Gather driftwood and useful materials
- Explore nearby coasts by small boat
- Preserve fish by smoking, salting and drying

## Disadvantages
- Very few skilled carpenters or metalworkers
- Limited ability to build permanent structures
- Heavily dependent on sea and weather
- Little inland agriculture experience

## Long-term Opportunities
Fish exports, dried and smoked seafood, salt production (if deposits found), pearl and shell gathering, coastal trade routes.

## Export
**Saltworks → Salt.** Salt is vital for preserving food, and the Crown pays well for it. See [[Crown Exports]].

## Crown Sells Them
Fishing equipment, nets, boats, salt, barrels, shipbuilding services. See [[Royal Supply Contracts]].
