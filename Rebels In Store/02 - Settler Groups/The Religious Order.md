# The Religious Order

A small group of priests, acolytes and healers sent to bring faith to the colony and care for its sick. See [[Settler Groups]].

**Leader:** [[Religious Leader - Agathe|Mother Agathe Morrow]]

## Equipment
They bring a portable altar, relics, holy water casks, an infirmary chest and the tools for making pendants. These need special stowage aboard [[The Royal Fortune]] and take up the space of 10 places, so the team and its equipment fill the room of a full settler group.

## Production
Produces **1 Faith per week** ([[Colony Production]]), because the work is slow. This is the Bastion *Craft* order, as in the Sanctuary facility.

## Healing Potions

Potions are brewed in **batches of 3**. The cost below is for the whole batch.

| Item | Faith cost (batch of 3) |
| --- | --- |
| [Potion of Healing](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:potion-of-healing) (2d4+2) | 1 |
| Potion of Greater Healing (4d4+4) | 3 |
| Potion of Superior Healing (8d4+8) | 6, needs a temple upgrade |

## Blessed Pendants (house rule)
A pendant holds one [Cleric](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/cleric:main) spell. The wearer can cast it **once per Long Rest** without using a spell slot. The spell save DC and attack bonus are **13 / +5**.

| Tier | Spell level | Example spells | Faith cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 1st | Bless, Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Shield of Faith, Sanctuary, Protection from Evil and Good | 3 |
| II | 2nd | Aid, Lesser Restoration, Prayer of Healing, Warding Bond | +3 (upgrade from I) |
| III | 3rd | Revivify, Remove Curse, Spirit Guardians | +5, needs a temple upgrade |

- **Upgrading:** an existing pendant can move up one tier. It keeps its spell or swaps it for one of the new level.
- **Limit:** each character can attune to one pendant. This keeps pendants from replacing the party's own spellcasters.

## Advantages
- The colony's only source of healing potions
- Keeps the settlers' morale and health up (plague events, injuries)
- Political weight with the Crown and its church

## Disadvantages
- Produce no Food or Production
- Have their own agenda and may clash with the alchemists or the Crown
- Tithes: they may ask for part of the colony's output

## Export
**Apiary and Chandlery → Beeswax candles and mead.** Churches and nobles back home use them in large amounts. See [[Crown Exports]].

## Crown Sells Them
Holy water, relics, vestments, religious texts, temple fittings. See [[Royal Supply Contracts]].
