## Medical Reserve

| Supply | Qty | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Potion of Healing](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:potion-of-healing) | 6 (2 batches of 3) | Emergencies only |
| Potion of Greater Healing | 3 (1 batch) | Emergencies only |
| Special scroll of *Revivify* | 1 | The last resort; anyone can use it, no roll needed |

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: The hidden potions
> These are **not on the public inventory**. [[Quartermaster - Isolde|Brannock]] keeps them locked away for a real emergency, and only she and [[Expedition Commander - Edric|Halloway]] know they exist.
> - She only admits they exist, let alone hands any over, if the players give her a **very good reason**: a life at stake right now, a threat to the whole colony, or a mission from Halloway where failure means disaster. A clever lie or a routine trip is not enough.
> - Even then, she wants a **DC 18 Charisma (Persuasion)** check, or Halloway's direct order, and she gives out as few as she can.
> - Once used, they're gone until the colony can make or buy more (see [[The Religious Order]] and [[Royal Supply Contracts#Trading the Surplus (proposed)|Trading the Surplus]]).

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: The last resort
> The **scroll of *Revivify*** is not in Brannock's stores. [[Expedition Commander - Edric|Halloway]] keeps it on his person, and **he is the only one who knows it exists**. Not even Brannock knows.
> - Only Halloway can give it to the party, and only for someone who has just died (*Revivify* works within 1 minute of death) when the loss would be a disaster for the colony or the mission.
> - **A special scroll:** a royal chaplain prepared it so **anyone can read it, with no ability check or spellcasting needed**. It works automatically.
> - Using it is a real sacrifice for him: it was meant for the Crown's representative, which is himself.
> - If the party ever learns he had it and kept it secret, it could earn their trust ("he saved it for us") or their anger ("who else did he let die?").
