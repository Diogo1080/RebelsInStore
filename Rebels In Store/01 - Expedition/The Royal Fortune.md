# The Royal Fortune

*(provisional name)*

A repurposed military carrack that served the Crown's navy through a succession of maritime conflicts. Sturdy and spacious, but old and slow. Built for war, adapted for exploration. Part of [[The Royal Expedition]].

## Characteristics

| Characteristic | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Carrack |
| Crew | 15 sailors |
| Passenger capacity | 50 |
| Armament | 1 cannon (main deck) |
| Ammunition | 8 cannonballs |
| Boats | 1 longboat, 1 small dinghy |
| Cargo capacity | Limited remaining space |
| Condition | Serviceable, but old |
| Navigation | Experienced [[Navigator]] and charts |

The cannon is mainly a deterrent against hostile vessels or large threats. Ammunition is limited and there is no dedicated artillery crew.

The Crown expects the ship eventually to return home with reports, discoveries and profitable cargo.

## Fate after landing (undecided)

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **A. Remains at the colony** | Anchored offshore: transport, storage, emergency evacuation. Costs maintenance and crew provisions. *(recommended)* |
| B. Returns to the Crown | Colony loses its link to the outside world until the next ship. |
| C. Dismantled | Broken up for timber to build housing and one Bastion special facility (see below). |

**Food cost** (see [[Colony Production#Food Upkeep]]):

| | Stays (A) | Returns home (B) | Dismantled (C) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People in the colony | 65 | 57 (a skeleton crew of 8 sails home; 7 sailors stay as settlers) | 65 (the whole crew become settlers) |
| Upkeep per week | 4 Food | 3 Food | 4 Food |
| Starting stores | 32 Food | 28 Food (the crew take 4 for the voyage) | 32 Food |

### Option C: Dismantling the Ship
The hull, masts and decks become building material. In return the colony gets:
- **Housing** for the whole colony, so no Production has to be spent on shelter early on.
- **One special facility** chosen from the [Bastion rules](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/bastions). It works as described there, except that it has no level requirement:

| Facility | Bastion order | Why it fits a ship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storehouse | Trade | Cargo hold and stores |
| Armory | Trade | The ship's weapons, the cannon and the 8 cannonballs |
| Barrack | Recruit | The crew become defenders |
| Workshop | Craft | The ship's carpentry tools and timber |
| Smithy | Craft | Ironwork salvaged from the hull |
| Library | Research | Charts, logbooks and the [[Navigator]]'s instruments |

**Consequences**
- The colony has no ship of its own; only the [[Royal Supply Contracts|Crown's supply ship]] connects it to home.
- The colony can't escape by sea.
- **The Crown is angered.** It has lost a valuable warship:
  - **Royal Debt:** the colony owes the Crown **4 TP** (1,000 gp) for the ship. It can be paid in gold or [[Crown Exports]].
  - **+1 Royal Tax step** stays in place until the debt is paid in full.
  - **Crown Distrust (permanent):** the Crown now watches the colony and its leaders more closely. Paying the debt does not end this.

> [!danger]- DM ONLY: The [[Navigator]]'s view
> Ashby publicly supports dismantling the ship. Secretly he wants it because the colony will then depend entirely on Crown shipments.

> [!quote] The [[Expedition Commander]]'s view
> The commander thinks the plan is ingenious and isn't entirely against it. They also know it will cost them the Crown's trust, because they are the one who has to answer for the lost ship. If the players argue for it well, the commander may agree.

Returning costs 4 Food once and saves 1 Food every week, so it pays for itself after 4 weeks. The price is losing the ship.

Option A makes the arrival of the [[Royal Supply Contracts|seasonal supply ship]] an event rather than a given.
