# Campaign Rules

## Source of Truth
All mechanics use the **D&D 2024 rules** as published on [dnd2024.wikidot.com](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/), unless a house rule below says otherwise.

When a note uses a rule (item, price, crafting, downtime, creature), it links to the relevant wikidot page.

## House Rules
Changes to the base rules made for this campaign. Each entry: what changes, why, and the original rule.

| Rule                                             | Change                                                                                | Reason                                                | Original                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Colony Production]]                            | Each settler group makes 2 of its resource per week                                   | Simple colony management                              | Adapted from [Bastions](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/bastions); available from level 1; each building takes its own order, and Maintain no longer applies to the whole Bastion |
| [[Colony Production#Food Upkeep\|Food Upkeep]]   | 1 Food feeds 20 people per week; shortages escalate from Hungry to Unrest to Starving | Survival pressure; makes keeping the ship a real cost | Abstracts the 2024 rules for food and Exhaustion                                      |
| [[The Religious Order\|Blessed Pendants]]        | Pendant holds 1 cleric spell, usable once per Long Rest, can be upgraded by tier      | Healing and support for the party from the colony     | New item; based on the Bastion Craft order                                            |
| [[Royal Supply Contracts\|Escalating Royal Tax]] | Tax rises with each request to the Crown                                              | Crown pressure and political tension                  | Not covered by the 2024 rules                                                         |

## Likely Areas for Adaptation
- Colony economy: settlement income, taxes, [[Royal Supply Contracts]]
- Crafting with local resources (see the iron-ore rule in [[Expedition Supplies]])
- Downtime activities used as colony work assignments
